- **The dual gate significantly reduced hallucination and unsafe answers (RQ1).** C5 had a hallucination rate of {{study.per_condition.C5.llm.hallucination|pct}}, against {{study.per_condition.C1.llm.hallucination|pct}} for vanilla RAG and {{study.per_condition.C2.llm.hallucination|pct}} for prompt-only safety. Its unsafe-answer rate on unanswerable questions was {{study.per_condition.C5.llm.unsafe_answer|pct}}, against {{study.per_condition.C1.llm.unsafe_answer|pct}} and {{study.per_condition.C2.llm.unsafe_answer|pct}}. All four tests had Holm *p* ≤ {{study.tests.mcnemar.7.pvalue_holm|p}}, and the result held in every sensitivity analysis (the claim-filter analysis re-tests hallucination only).
- **The gain comes from selective abstention, not from more faithful answers.** Among responses that contained claims, hallucination stayed at {{study.per_condition.C5.llm.hallucination_given_claims|pct}} for C5 against {{study.per_condition.C1.llm.hallucination_given_claims|pct}} for C1. Coverage of answerable questions fell from {{study.per_condition.C1.coverage_answerable|pct}} to {{study.per_condition.C5.coverage_answerable|pct}}.
- **The Evidence Gate carries most of the effect (RQ2).** On its own it lowered unsafe answers to {{study.per_condition.C4.llm.unsafe_answer|pct}}. The Context Gate's added benefit was small and significant only per turn; it mainly turns refusals into clarification questions. Every emergency was escalated ({{study.per_condition.C5.emergency.recall_system.k|int}}/{{study.per_condition.C5.emergency.recall_system.n|int}}), with no false escalations.
- **A grounding verifier helps, at a cost in usefulness; a calibrated Evidence Gate does not (RQ3).** V2 lowered hallucination to {{study.per_condition.V2.llm.hallucination|pct}} (Holm *p* = {{study.tests.mcnemar.11.pvalue_holm|p}} against C5), largely by shortening answers ({{study.per_condition.V2.llm.claims_per_response|f1}} against {{study.per_condition.C5.llm.claims_per_response|f1}} claims per response); it did not significantly reduce unsafe answers and lowered coverage to {{study.per_condition.V2.coverage_answerable|pct}}. Now on by default, it can leave a one-sentence or abruptly starting reply. V1 had no significant effect (Holm *p* = {{study.tests.mcnemar.10.pvalue_holm|p}}) and stays off.
- **Routing and the evaluation remain the weak points.** C5 routed {{study.per_condition.C5.routing_system.accuracy_strict|pct}} of HalluBench turns exactly and the live AQ run {{aq.metrics.overall_routing_accuracy|perc}} of its vignettes (citation precision {{aq.metrics.overall_citation_precision|perc}}), most often at the ANSWER–GENERALIZE boundary ({{routing_errors.C5.answer_generalize|int}} of {{routing_errors.C5.misroutes|int}} HalluBench and {{routing_errors.aq.answer_generalize|int}} of {{routing_errors.aq.misroutes|int}} AQ misroutes), followed by abstentions on expected answers. Every AQ emergency was escalated, and no out-of-domain question received an ANSWER ({{aq.metrics.out_of_domain_abstain_count|int}} of {{aq.metrics.out_of_domain_count|int}} abstained). Only one judge scored the gated conditions, the judges agreed poorly at claim level, and human labels are outstanding.
- **Post-study robustness: one fix needed; the adversarial and smoke checks passed.** Live RABBITS exposed brand-name fragility; after the fix action consistency rose from {{rabbits_before.action_consistency_pct|perc}} to {{rabbits.action_consistency_pct|perc}} and stored-generic consistency from {{rabbits_before.extraction_consistency_pct|perc}} to {{rabbits.extraction_consistency_pct|perc}} (both runs with Gate 3 off; {{rabbits_counts.after.flips|int}} of the {{rabbits_counts.after.items|int}} items still flip, {{rabbits_counts.after.brand_more_cautious|int}} towards the more cautious action for the brand). All {{adversarial.counts.payloads|int}} adversarial payloads passed before and after, and the final smoke test passed {{smoke.counts.PASS|int}} of {{smoke.checks|len}} checks.
- **Latency improved only in part.** With the same model, the lifestyle answer fell from {{perf.before_gemma3-4b.warm_summary.S5-t1:ANSWER.p50|f1}} s to {{perf.after_gemma3-4b.warm_summary.S5-t1:ANSWER.p50|f1}} s warm, but the ABSTAIN path rose from {{perf.before_gemma3-4b.warm_summary.S2-t1:ABSTAIN.p50|f1}} s to {{perf.after_gemma3-4b.warm_summary.S2-t1:ABSTAIN.p50|f1}} s. Model calls take almost all of the turn time, and one local model server serialises concurrent users.
