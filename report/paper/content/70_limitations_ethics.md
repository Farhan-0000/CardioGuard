# Limitations and Ethics {#sec:limitations}

**Limitations.** The benchmark is synthetic: items, gold actions and notes were drafted by the team with the help of an AI assistant and have not yet been reviewed by clinicians, so the labels encode the authors' reading of the guidelines, and a few borderline items admit another defensible label. Its phrasing is author-approximated (one Hinglish item), and the line between answerable and unanswerable depends on the corpus at labelling time. Hallucination is measured by automatic judges: the local LLM judge is of moderate size (in development, a smaller judge changed a claim's label when the other claims in its batch changed), and the NLI judge is calibrated against it, so the two are not fully independent; agreement with human annotators is still to be measured. Results come from one application model, one judge and one hardware platform, and the paired tests treat turns of a conversation as independent, which the item-level analysis only partly addresses. The corpus is English and mainly international or Western, without an Indian national guideline. The emergency rules were developed on cases written by the same team, and the system has not been evaluated with patients.

**Ethics and privacy.** MedBridge is a research prototype for health information, not a medical device: it does not diagnose or prescribe, refers treatment decisions to the patient's doctor and directs suspected emergencies to emergency services.
