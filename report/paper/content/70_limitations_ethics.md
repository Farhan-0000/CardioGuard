**Limitations.** *Judges.* Every result for a gated condition rests on one automatic judge; the NLI judge scored only ungated outputs, and the human labels are pending. The LLM judge labels restated user facts and generic hedges as unsupported, which inflates absolute rates in every condition (paired comparisons are less affected), and template responses count as free of hallucination, so response-level rates mix abstention with grounding. *Benchmark.* HalluBench is synthetic, drafted by the team with the help of an AI assistant and not reviewed by clinicians. With only {{study.per_condition.C5.llm.unsafe_answer.n}} unanswerable test turns the unsafe-answer intervals are wide, and the paired tests treat turns of a conversation as independent, which the item-level analysis only partly addresses. *Protocol.* Each condition ran once, with one application model, judge and hardware platform; V2's thresholds were chosen on {{calibration.v2.dev_result.n_turns}} development turns to agree with the same LLM judge that scored the test split; C3 and C4 against C1G and V12 against V2 were not tested. *Provenance.* C0–C5 and V1 ran almost entirely at commit 82359eb, C1G partly at 085eb53, and V2, V12 and the rest of C1G at 3f58b59, which only adds a process-wide PyTorch inference lock (Gate 3 and the reranker had crashed the MPS backend when run concurrently); every run had uncommitted changes. The brand-name fix (02d9774), the Gate 3 default (7a3bc67) and its withdrawal rule (16e594b) came later, so the deployed system is not the evaluated one: RABBITS was re-run after the fix with Gate 3 off, and the withdrawal rule is unmeasured. The corpus is English, without an Indian national guideline; the emergency rules were developed on the team's own cases; and the system has not been evaluated with patients.

**Ethics and privacy.** MedBridge is a research prototype for health information, not a medical device: it does not diagnose or prescribe and refers treatment decisions and emergencies to professionals. No human participants or real patient records were involved.
