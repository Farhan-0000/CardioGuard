# Conclusion {#sec:conclusion}

MedBridge treats answerability as a first-class decision in patient-facing clinical RAG: a deterministic emergency path, a pre-retrieval Context Gate and a post-retrieval Evidence Gate decide whether to answer, ask, generalise, abstain or escalate, and the riskiest outcomes involve no generated text. The system runs on local open-weight models sized for a laptop GPU and is evaluated on MedBridge-HalluBench with ablations, baselines, two complementary judges and paired exact tests under a calibration-gated protocol.
