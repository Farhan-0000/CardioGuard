# Related Work {#sec:related}

**Grounding and hallucination mitigation.** Beyond plain RAG [@lewis2020rag], Self-RAG fine-tunes a generator to emit reflection tokens that decide when to retrieve and whether a segment is supported [@asai2024selfrag], and Corrective RAG scores retrieved documents and refines or replaces them when confidence is low [@yan2024crag]. Other methods check text after it is written: Chain-of-Verification answers self-posed verification questions [@dhuliawala2024cove], RARR retrieves evidence per claim and revises unsupported content [@gao2023rarr], and SelfCheckGPT flags sentences that are inconsistent across samples [@manakul2023selfcheckgpt]. FActScore measures factual precision over atomic facts [@min2023factscore]. These approaches need a specially trained generator, still answer every input, or act only after generation. MedBridge decides *before* generation whether an answer may be produced at all; its optional Gate 3 follows the post-hoc idea but can only delete unsupported sentences, regenerate an answer as general information, or withdraw it.

**Selective prediction and abstention.** Selective classification rejects inputs whose confidence falls below a threshold calibrated to bound the risk on accepted inputs [@geifman2017selective], and a recent survey organises LLM abstention by query, model and human values [@wen2025abstention]. Such methods yield one answer-or-refuse signal. MedBridge separates two reasons not to answer that need different replies, missing personal context (ask) and insufficient evidence (generalise or abstain), and adds escalation; its calibrated variant borrows the selective-prediction idea for reranker scores.

**Evaluating medical LLMs.** Medical LLM benchmarks such as MultiMedQA and MIRAGE consist mainly of exam-style questions (with clinician-rated long-form answers in MultiMedQA), and every question expects an answer [@singhal2023llmclinical; @xiong2024medrag]; ours labels each turn with the expected behaviour, including asking, abstaining and escalating. We use an LLM judge [@zheng2023judging], but because LLM judges can favour their own generations [@zheng2023judging; @panickssery2024llm], it comes from a different family than the application model and is paired with a deterministic NLI judge.

**Patents and security.** Patented prior art includes a question-answering system that can return an explicit "no answer" [@bhagwat2017ibmnoanswer], LLM detection of ambiguous queries followed by a clarifying question [@zhang2024intuitdisambiguity], and RAG-based clinical decision support over a curated guideline library [@paul2025glasshealthrag]. In the documents we reviewed, none combines a pre-retrieval context gate, a post-retrieval evidence gate and a deterministic pre-model emergency override with template bypass. RAG applications are also exposed to prompt injection, including indirect injection through retrieved content [@greshake2023notwhat], which motivates the input isolation in [@sec:method].
